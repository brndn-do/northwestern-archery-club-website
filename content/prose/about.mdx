Northwestern University Archery Club is a student group founded in 2013. We exist to give
anyone at Northwestern a way to pick up a bow, whether they have shot before or not.

We are **recreational and beginner-focused**. We are not a competitive travel team, and you do
not need to work toward one. Most people come to practice, shoot for ninety minutes, and go
home having enjoyed themselves.

## What we shoot

Our club equipment is **recurve barebow** — a recurve bow shot without sights or stabilizers.
It is a forgiving style to learn on, it rewards consistency over gear, and it is what our
coaching is built around. If you own equipment in another style, talk to an exec member.

## Who runs practice

Most of our officers are USA Archery-certified Instructors. If it is your first time shooting,
one of them will walk you through the basics before you step on the line. If you have shot
before, they are still worth asking for a second pair of eyes.

## What a practice looks like

Practices run ninety minutes at Blomquist Recreation Center. You check in with an exec member,
sign a waiver if you need one, and get a safety briefing if it is your first time. After that
it is mostly shooting, with coaching available whenever you want it.
